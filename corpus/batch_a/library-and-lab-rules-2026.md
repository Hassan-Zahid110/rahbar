# Margalla Institute of Technology: Library and Computing Lab Regulations (2026)

## 1. Library Borrowing Privileges

1.1 Undergraduate students may borrow up to 4 circulating books at a time for a loan period of 14 calendar days.

1.2 Graduate (MS and PhD) students may borrow up to 6 circulating books at a time for a loan period of 21 calendar days. Reference volumes and bound journals may not be checked out.

## 2. Overdue Fines and Lost Books

2.1 An overdue fine of PKR 50 per book per calendar day is charged for late returns. Borrowing privileges are suspended whenever unpaid library fines exceed PKR 1,000.

2.2 A student who loses or damages a library book must pay twice (2x) the current market price of the book plus a non-refundable PKR 500 cataloging and processing charge.

## 3. Computing Laboratory Usage

3.1 Computing labs are open from 08:00 to 21:00 Monday through Saturday. Installing unauthorized software, cryptocurrency miners, or network packet sniffers results in an immediate 30-day account suspension.
