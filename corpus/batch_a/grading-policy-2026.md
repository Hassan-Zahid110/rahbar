# Margalla Institute of Technology: Grading and Assessment Policy (2026)

## 1. Letter Grades and Grade Points

1.1 Course performance is evaluated on a 4.00 grade point scale: Grade A and A+ carry 4.00 grade points, A- carries 3.67, B+ carries 3.33, B carries 3.00, B- carries 2.67, C+ carries 2.33, C carries 2.00, C- carries 1.67, D+ carries 1.33, D carries 1.00, and F carries 0.00 grade points.

1.2 The grade FA (Failure due to Attendance) carries 0.00 grade points and is treated identically to an F grade in GPA computations.

## 2. Incomplete Grade (Grade I)

2.1 An Incomplete grade (I) may be awarded only when a student with at least 80 percent attendance misses the final examination due to verifiable hospitalization or immediate family bereavement.

2.2 All Incomplete (I) grades must be converted into a regular letter grade within the first 4 weeks of the next regular semester; otherwise, the grade automatically converts to F.

## 3. Grade Appeal and Rechecking

3.1 A student may submit a formal final examination rechecking request within 7 calendar days of grade publication by paying a rechecking fee of PKR 2,000 per course. Rechecking verifies unmarked questions and arithmetic totaling only.
