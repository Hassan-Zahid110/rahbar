# Margalla Institute of Technology: Final Year Project Regulations (2026)

## 1. Eligibility and Registration

1.1 An undergraduate student is eligible to register for Final Year Project Part I (FYP-I) only after completing at least 90 credit hours with a minimum CGPA of 2.00.

1.2 The Final Year Project spans two consecutive regular semesters: FYP-I (3 credit hours) and FYP-II (3 credit hours), totaling 6 credit hours.

## 2. Supervision and Group Size

2.1 Each FYP team must consist of 2 to 3 students registered in the same degree programme. Solo projects require written approval from the Departmental FYP Committee.

2.2 Every FYP team is assigned a full-time faculty supervisor. A faculty member may supervise at most 5 active FYP groups simultaneously.

## 3. Plagiarism and Deliverable Evaluation

3.1 The final thesis report and software repository must undergo Turnitin similarity screening prior to defense. The overall similarity index must be strictly below 15 percent, with no single source exceeding 3 percent.
