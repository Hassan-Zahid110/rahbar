# Margalla Institute of Technology: Scholarship and Financial Aid Policy (2026)

## 1. Merit Scholarships

1.1 Merit scholarships are awarded each regular semester to the top 3 students in each degree batch who complete at least 15 credit hours and achieve a Semester GPA (SGPA) of 3.80 or higher.

1.2 The first-ranked student receives a 100 percent tuition fee waiver, the second-ranked student receives a 75 percent tuition waiver, and the third-ranked student receives a 50 percent tuition waiver for the subsequent semester.

## 2. Need-Based Financial Aid (Qarz-e-Hasna)

2.1 Need-based financial assistance is provided as an interest-free study loan (Qarz-e-Hasna) covering between 25 percent and 100 percent of tuition fees.

2.2 To retain need-based financial aid in subsequent semesters, a recipient must maintain a minimum CGPA of 2.50 and have zero disciplinary violations or F/FA grades.

## 3. Dean's Honour List

3.1 Any undergraduate student completing at least 15 credit hours in a regular semester with an SGPA of 3.50 or above and no grade below C is placed on the Dean's Honour List.
