# Margalla Institute of Technology: Graduation and Academic Medals Policy (2026)

## 1. Undergraduate Degree Completion Requirements

1.1 To qualify for the award of a BS degree, a student must successfully complete 130 to 136 prescribed credit hours with a minimum cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of 2.00, zero unresolved F or I grades, and 40 hours of verified community service.

## 2. Convocation Medals (Gold, Silver, and Bronze)

2.1 Gold, Silver, and Bronze medals are awarded to the first, second, and third highest CGPA graduates in each discipline who completed their degree within the normal 8-semester duration with a minimum CGPA of 3.75 and never repeated, failed, or withdrew from any course.
