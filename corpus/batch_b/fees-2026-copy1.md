# Margalla Institute of Technology: Tuition and Fee Schedule (Fall 2026)

## 1. Tuition Fees

1.1 The tuition fee for BS undergraduate programmes across all disciplines is PKR 10,000 per credit hour.

1.2 The tuition fee for MS graduate programmes is PKR 12,500 per credit hour, and for PhD programmes is PKR 15,000 per credit hour.

1.3 A non-refundable one-time admission fee of PKR 30,000 is payable at initial enrollment, along with a refundable security deposit of PKR 20,000.

## 2. Administrative and Auxiliary Charges

| Charge Type | Amount (PKR) | Billing Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Late registration fee | 3,000 | Per semester after deadline |
| Official transcript | 1,500 | Per sealed copy |
| Campus transfer fee | 5,000 | Per approved transfer request |
| Student activity and lab fee | 6,000 | Per regular semester |

## 3. Tuition Refund Policy

3.1 Tuition is refunded in full (100 percent) when a course or semester registration is dropped within the first 10 calendar days of classes.

3.2 A 50 percent tuition refund is granted when a course is dropped between day 11 and day 20 of classes. No tuition refund is granted after day 20 of the semester.
