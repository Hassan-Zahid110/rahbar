# Margalla Institute of Technology: Academic Rules (2023 Edition)

## 1. Attendance

1.1 A student must attend at least 75 percent of lectures to sit the final examination.

## 2. Academic Warning

2.1 A student whose CGPA falls below 1.75 is placed on academic warning.
