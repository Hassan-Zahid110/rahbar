# Margalla Institute of Technology: Course Registration and Add-Drop Policy (2026)

## 1. Standard Credit Hour Load and Overload

1.1 The normal course load for a full-time undergraduate student in a regular semester is 15 to 18 credit hours.

1.2 A student with a CGPA of 3.25 or higher, or a graduating senior in their final semester, may register for one additional course up to a maximum overload of 21 credit hours with Head of Department approval.

## 2. Add/Drop Period and Prerequisites

2.1 Students may add or drop courses without transcript notation or financial penalty until the Friday of Week 2 of a regular semester. No course may be taken unless all listed prerequisite courses have been passed with at least grade D.
