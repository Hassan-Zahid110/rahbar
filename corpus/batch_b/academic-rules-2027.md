# Margalla Institute of Technology: Academic Rules (2027 Revised Edition)

This 2027 edition supersedes the 2026 edition for all degree programmes effective Spring 2027.

## 1. Attendance

1.1 A student must attend at least 80 percent of the scheduled lectures and laboratory sessions of each course to be eligible to sit the final examination. The Head of Department may condone up to 10 percent further absence for documented hospital admission.

1.2 A student who falls below the minimum 80 percent attendance threshold receives the grade FA (Failure due to Attendance) in that course, which carries 0.00 grade points.

## 2. Academic Warning and Probation

2.1 A student whose cumulative grade point average (CGPA) falls below 2.00 at the end of any regular semester is placed on academic warning.

2.2 A student on academic warning may register for at most 12 credit hours in the following regular semester. Under the revised 2027 rules, a student who remains on academic warning for two consecutive regular semesters (reduced from three in 2026) is dropped from the degree programme.

## 3. Repeating a Course and Course Withdrawal

3.1 A student may repeat any course in which they earned a grade below B (3.00) to improve their CGPA. Only the grade of the most recent attempt counts toward the CGPA, even if it is lower than an earlier attempt.

3.2 A student may withdraw from a course between Week 3 and Week 10 of a regular semester. A withdrawn course is recorded with the grade W on the transcript and does not affect the SGPA or CGPA.

## 4. Maximum Degree Duration

4.1 The maximum duration permitted to complete a four-year undergraduate BS degree programme is 7 academic years (14 regular semesters), inclusive of any semester freezes or leaves of absence.
